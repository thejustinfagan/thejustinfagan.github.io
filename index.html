fleet-intel

Fleet Intel is the working commercial-intelligence platform for finding and evaluating trucking fleets, facilities, and sales opportunities across the United States.

How it is used: combines carrier authority and safety records, facility evidence, maps and imagery, company matching, account context, and report generation so a large market becomes a prioritized field plan.

Timeline-native gamesPrivate

Battle_Dinghy

Battle Dinghy is a multiplayer Battleship-style game designed to run through social posts rather than inside a conventional game client.

How it is used: manages challenges, turns, shots, persistent game state, visual board renders, player statistics, and the bot behavior required to operate a complete match on the timeline.

Testing infrastructurePrivate

threadplay-simulator

A pixel-faithful social-network simulator for testing real ThreadPlay application code without depending on a live platform or production account.

How it is used: provides an X-compatible fake API, repeatable multiplayer scenarios, deterministic test runs, and realistic marketing captures for Battle Dinghy and future ThreadPlay games.

Public-record intelligencePrivate

real-audit

RealAudit is a national, CLI-first diligence engine for counterparty, property, and real-estate deal research using public records.

How it is used: turns source-backed records into findings, severity and confidence scores, limitations, red flags, and recommended next checks while keeping professional verification explicit.

Opportunity intelligencePrivate

signal-foundry

Signal Foundry converts saved external evidence into structured opportunity intelligence rather than leaving it as an unsearchable bookmark archive.

How it is used: captures source trails, segments signals, records contradictions, extracts reusable capabilities, identifies macro patterns, and routes evidence toward active workflows.

Discovery productPrivate

swipenft

SwipeNFT is a mobile-first discovery product for browsing curated NFT collections and artists with a simple swipe interaction.

How it is used: runs separate PFP and Tezos-artist editions from one codebase, publishes curated weekly pools, tracks likes, and keeps live marketplace dependencies out of the swipe path.
